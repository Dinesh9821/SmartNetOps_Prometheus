Audit readiness

Evidence collected against evidence required, per control family. The gap is the work outstanding before the next audit window.

Control coverage by framework

The same estate assessed against each framework it is bound by.

Policy exceptions

Accepted risks with an owner and an expiry. An expired exception is an unowned risk.

Data residency

Where regulated data physically sits, against where policy says it may sit.

Asset attestation

Assets with a confirmed owner and a current review date.

Contractual commitments

Every SLA the organisation has signed, and current attainment against it. Breach exposure is stated in credits, not percentages.

Requires sign-off

Items awaiting an accountable decision.
